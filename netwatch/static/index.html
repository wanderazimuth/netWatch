    Who's home, last 7 days

    How it works

    Someone is home while one of their phones (or watches) is on the network: a device whose Person is them and whose Type is Phone or Wearable. Laptops and TVs don't count, as they stay home when people leave. Phones go quiet on Wi-Fi while asleep, so someone only counts as away once all their phones have been gone for . A shorter time catches quick errands but may call a sleeping phone away.

      How it works

      Every scan checks which MAC address answers for the router's IP, in the scan and in the Pi's own ARP cache. A new one can mean a device is posing as the router to see others' traffic (ARP spoofing). The same scan notices two devices answering for one IP. Every 15 minutes the Pi asks for a DHCP offer, without taking an address, to find any DHCP server besides the router. Each problem alerts once at high priority, and again only if it goes away for a day and comes back.

      Ookla server IDs, tried in order, then Ookla's own pick. Ookla picks the lowest ping, which isn't always a server fast enough for the line. Set: SUMOFIBER (2185), then CenturyLink (8863).
      Before each test the Deco is asked how much every device is using. Cameras and smart-home gear don't count. "Run test now" doesn't wait.
